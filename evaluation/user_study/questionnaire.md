# Post-task questionnaire (5-point Likert: 1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree)

| # | Construct | Item |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Usefulness | The analysis was useful for thinking through this decision. |
| 2 | Transparency | I understand why the options were evaluated the way they were. |
| 3 | Explanation quality | The explanations were clear and specific to this dilemma. |
| 4 | Trust calibration | The stated uncertainty matched how confident I should be in the analysis. |
| 5 | Trust calibration (reverse) | I felt pressured to accept the system's suggestion. |
| 6 | Consistency | I expect the system would give a similar analysis if I described the dilemma differently. |
| 7 | Ease of understanding | The output was easy to understand. |
| 8 | Cultural relevance | The cultural/philosophical perspective was relevant to the dilemma. |
| 9 | Cultural respect | The cultural/philosophical perspective was presented accurately and respectfully. |
| 10 | Decision confidence | I feel more confident in my own decision after using the system. |
| 11 | Autonomy | I felt that the final decision remained mine. |
| 12 | Willingness to use | I would use this system for a real decision. |

Items 8–9 are shown only for conditions that include a cultural perspective; record "not applicable" otherwise.
The DharmaNet decision screen collects items 1, 2, 4, 8 and 10 in-app (`/api/analyses/{id}/feedback`).
